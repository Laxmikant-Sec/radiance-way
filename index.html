The meaning behind our name

What Does Radiance Mean?

Radiance is the light that emerges when inner wisdom, courage, and compassion are expressed through our daily actions.

Inspired by the Lotus Sutra's teaching of the potential for Buddhahood in all people, Radiance symbolizes the possibility of awakening our highest qualities, even during difficult times.

Wisdom

Discovering clarity, understanding, and hope in everyday life.

Courage

Facing challenges with determination and transforming obstacles into growth.

Compassion

Respecting the dignity of life and encouraging happiness in ourselves and others.

Our inspiration

The Lotus Sutra and Human Potential

The Lotus Sutra emphasizes the profound potential of every person to attain Buddhahood.

Nichiren Buddhism encourages individuals to bring forth their inner strength, develop wisdom and compassion, and create value amid life's challenges.

Radiance shares reflections inspired by these teachings, inviting thoughtful study, dialogue, and positive action.

The light of one awakened life can become a source of hope for many others.
