About RadianceWay Cybersecurity

Protecting Your Digital Future

Small businesses deserve practical, understandable cybersecurity tailored to their needs. That's the idea behind RadianceWay Cybersecurity, a Winnipeg-based initiative founded by Laxmikant Sharma

A graduate of Robertson College's Cyber and Cloud Security Professional program, Laxmikant brings hands-on experience in network security, firewall configuration, security monitoring, and IT infrastructure.

At RadianceWay Cybersecurity, we believe effective security starts with understanding the risks, communicating clearly, and focusing on practical solutions. Our goal is to help small businesses strengthen their defenses, protect their systems, and safeguard valuable information without making cybersecurity unnecessarily complicated.

Our Mission

Making Cybersecurity Clear and Accessible

Our mission is to help small businesses understand cybersecurity risks and strengthen their defenses through practical guidance, security awareness, and clear, actionable recommendations.

RadianceWay represents clarity and guidance in an increasingly complex digital world. We aim to help businesses identify potential security risks, understand their options, and take practical steps toward stronger protection.

Security Awareness

Building awareness of phishing, suspicious activity, and common cyber threats so businesses are better prepared to recognize potential risks.

Network Security

Reviewing network security, firewall configurations, and security settings to identify areas where protection can be strengthened.

Security Monitoring

Improving visibility into security activity through log monitoring and threat detection, helping businesses identify events that may require attention.

Our Cybersecurity Approach

Building Trust Through Stronger Security

At RadianceWay Cybersecurity, we believe effective cybersecurity begins with understanding the risks. Our approach focuses on identifying potential vulnerabilities, reviewing existing protections, and helping small businesses make informed security decisions.

We emphasize proactive security practices, including network protection, security awareness, monitoring, and continuous improvement. By identifying potential weaknesses early, businesses can take practical steps to reduce risks and build a stronger security foundation.

Every business has different security needs. Through clear communication, practical recommendations, and thoughtful security improvements, we aim to help small businesses strengthen their defenses and approach cybersecurity with greater confidence.

Strong cybersecurity begins with awareness, grows through preparation, and builds confidence through protection.
