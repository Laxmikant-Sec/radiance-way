Our Cybersecurity Services

Every business faces different cybersecurity challenges. RadianceWay Cybersecurity focuses on practical security reviews, awareness, and recommendations to help small businesses understand and reduce their digital risks.

01. Cybersecurity Health Checks

A structured review of common cybersecurity practices, including account security, software updates, device protection, and basic access controls.

The goal is to identify potential weaknesses and provide clear, prioritized recommendations for improvement.

02. Email Security & Phishing Awareness

Email remains a common entry point for cyber threats. We focus on recognizing suspicious messages, understanding phishing techniques, and reviewing practical email protection measures.

Recommendations may include multi-factor authentication, account protection practices, and employee awareness.

03. Network Security Reviews

A practical examination of network security fundamentals, including firewall configurations, wireless security, network segmentation, and device exposure.

The objective is to identify areas where network protection can be strengthened and unnecessary risks can be reduced.

04. Security Monitoring & Log Reviews

Security logs can provide valuable information about authentication attempts, system activity, and potentially suspicious events.

We focus on reviewing available logs, identifying events that may require attention, and explaining findings in understandable terms.

Start with a Conversation

Not every business needs the same level of cybersecurity support. An initial discussion can help clarify your concerns and determine which type of review or guidance may be appropriate.
